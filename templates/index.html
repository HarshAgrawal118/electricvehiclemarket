Project Overview

Problem Statement & Data Preprocessing Pipeline

01. Problem Statement

Making Sense of Rapid Electric Vehicle Market Growth

As the automotive sector transitions toward zero-emission mobility, decision-makers, manufacturers, and consumers face vast, fragmented datasets. Raw registration records make it difficult to compare Battery Electric Vehicles (BEVs) against Plug-in Hybrids (PHEVs), track real-world electric range gains, or identify high-growth regions.

This project transforms raw EV market records into an interactive Tableau Dashboard and guided Data Story embedded inside a Python Flask web application.

02. Data Preprocessing

Preparing Clean, Reliable Data for Visualization

Data Cleaning

Handled null values in electric range and MSRP fields, and removed duplicate vehicle entries.

Standardization

Normalized manufacturer names, model years, and BEV/PHEV classifications for accurate grouping.

Geo-Formatting

Validated state, county, and city fields so regional adoption maps render cleanly in Tableau.

Calculated Metrics

Created custom KPIs for average range per charge, market share %, and year-over-year growth.

Key Findings & Insights

Data-Driven Takeaways & Strategic Recommendations from the EV Market Analysis

Finding 01

Exponential Market Acceleration

Electric vehicle adoption shows a steep upward inflection across recent model years. Growth shifted from early-adopter niche volumes to mainstream mass-market expansion, fueled by stricter emission mandates and expanding model choices.

Finding 02

Clear Consumer Shift Toward Pure BEVs

Battery Electric Vehicles (BEVs) hold the dominant market share over Plug-in Hybrid Electric Vehicles (PHEVs). Consumers increasingly favor zero-tailpipe, all-electric drivetrains over transitional hybrid powertrains.

Finding 03

Battery Range Gains Reduce Range Anxiety

Average electric driving range per single charge has risen consistently in newer model years. Higher battery capacity paired with competitive pricing directly correlates with surges in consumer registration volume.

Finding 04

High Brand & Regional Concentration

Market leadership remains heavily concentrated among pioneering EV manufacturers and urban/coastal regions with mature charging networks, while emerging regions show the highest percentage growth potential.

Actionable Business & Policy Insights

Charging Infrastructure

Prioritize fast-charging corridors in high-registration counties and emerging suburban markets to sustain BEV adoption momentum.

Affordability & Mid-Tier Models

Automakers expanding into mid-range MSRP segments capture the largest volume growth as early luxury demand saturates.

Targeted Policy Incentives

Focusing tax credits and clean-fuel eligibility on long-range BEVs accelerates fleet decarbonization more effectively than broad subsidies.
